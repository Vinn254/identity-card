About this project

Identification Management System for Lost and Found Documents is a senior project (INSY 492) developed at the University of Eastern Africa, Baraton — School of Business, Department of Information Systems and Computing.

It provides a centralized, secure web platform for the university community to report lost or found identification documents, search the system database, and be automatically notified when a match is found.
